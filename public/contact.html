Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, reassuring answers about our personal safety and violence prevention approach. If you don't see your question here, we're always happy to talk.

Is this training only for beginners?

Not at all. Our personal protection programs are built for every experience level. Whether you're completely new to safety awareness or have some background, we adapt the material to meet you where you are. The focus is always on practical, real-world readiness — not athletic performance or previous training history.

Do I need to be athletic or physically fit?

No. Our methodology centers on prevention, awareness, and verbal de-escalation — not physical confrontation. While we do cover protective physical skills, they're designed to be accessible regardless of age, fitness level, or physical ability. This is about intelligent personal safety, not gym conditioning.

Are programs suitable for children or families together?

Absolutely. We offer age-appropriate safety education for young people and family-oriented sessions where parents and children learn together. These programs emphasize awareness, boundary-setting, and trusted communication — delivered in a calm, reassuring manner that strengthens family confidence without introducing fear.

Can organizations request corporate violence prevention training workshops?

Yes. We regularly deliver tailored workshops for corporations, real estate agencies, healthcare teams, schools, and community groups. Each session addresses the specific risks your people face — from client meetings in isolated settings to workplace harassment and travel safety. Contact us to discuss a program built around your team's real-world environment.

What makes this approach different from martial arts classes?

We are Personal Protection & Violence Prevention Specialists — not a martial arts school. Our focus is entirely on real-world safety: recognizing predatory behavior, managing fear, using your voice, and making fast, intelligent decisions under pressure. There are no belts, no tournaments, and no sport techniques. Just practical, experience-based preparation for the situations that actually happen.

Still have questions? We're here to help you feel prepared and confident.
